# **App Name**: ProfitPro AI

## Core Features:

- Website Analysis: Analyze user's existing website or idea to identify potential revenue streams and areas for improvement.
- Revenue Maximization Tool: Provide a detailed plan with actionable steps to maximize income from the user's idea, leveraging industry best practices. Uses AI as a tool to incorporate domain specific data, financial information, and other trends into its decision-making.
- Tiered Access (Free, Pro, Beast Mode): Implement a subscription model with three tiers: Free (basic analysis), Pro ($14.99/month - enhanced analysis & reporting), and Beast Mode ($29.99/month - premium AI coaching & predictions).
- AI Coach (Beast Mode): An AI-powered financial advisor/CEO persona whose sole focus is maximizing the user's profits, offering personalized guidance and strategies.
- Predictive Analysis (Beast Mode): Offer revenue projections and success probabilities based on the user's execution of the provided plan, using advanced machine learning models. The tool will be a blend of past data from top similar markets, new strategies implemented by early adopters in certain markets to gauge future performance and sentiment anlalysis and trends data.
- Viral Platform Integration: Identify and recommend the best platforms and strategies to reach the maximum audience, including emerging trends and viral sites. Has access to resources such as new social networks, and growing online platforms.
- Integrated Organizer: Provide an integrated organizational tool with tasks management to ensure plan implementation is organized and structured for achieving goals

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep indigo (#4B0082), evoking professionalism and insight.
- Background color: Very light indigo (#F0E6FF), creating a clean and uncluttered interface.
- Accent color: Vibrant violet (#8A2BE2) used to highlight key insights and calls to action.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif, for a modern and technical feel. Body font: 'Inter', sans-serif.
- Use a consistent set of financial and growth-oriented icons.
- A clean and structured layout with clear sections for analysis, plans, and predictions.
- Subtle animations on data loading and plan updates.
